# Code of Conduct

SLOG is a small technical project, and participation should remain useful,
welcoming, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of hardware and software experience.
- State uncertainty, especially around electrical, thermal, and human safety.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing another person's data or
  identifying context.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publication of private data
are not acceptable. Neither is pressuring someone to disclose measurements,
installation details, or personal sauna practices.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project-run spaces,
and public representation of the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact options
on [@akkauppi's GitHub profile](https://github.com/akkauppi). Do not publish
sensitive details in an issue. For abuse occurring on GitHub, GitHub's own
reporting tools are also available. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
